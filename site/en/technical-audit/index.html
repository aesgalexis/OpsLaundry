Technical audits for industrial laundries

We inspect the laundry while it is running to find what slows production, what is going wrong and what needs attention first.

Production and linen flow

We observe loads, cycle times, waiting times and backlogs between machines. We compare machine capacity with the output the line actually achieves.

Equipment and maintenance

We review machine condition and settings, recurring faults, preventive maintenance and critical spare parts. We speak to the people who operate and maintain the equipment.

A report to organise the work

You receive a technical report by area, with findings and proposed actions: what to address first, what to schedule and what needs further investigation or investment.

Where does your laundry get held up?

Tell us what happens, where in the process and how long it has been happening. This helps us define the scope of the audit.
